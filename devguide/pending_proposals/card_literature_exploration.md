## Source and interpretation contract

The shared `literature` topic works across Card natures. It reuses
`Card.literature()` and `Card.explain_literature(publication_ref)` over detached
captured input; browsing acquires no source, article or metadata. The only
scientific option is the existing exact `publication_ref`, with no identity join
by title, name or similarity.

`literature_exploration@1` preserves the complete original overview, publication
order and focused explanation. Citations, primary structure citations, ECO
support, curated statements, mentions, structure mentions, source activity records
and supplementary article metadata keep their own categories. A count of activity
records is not a count of independent experiments. A mention is not support for
an arbitrary claim or Nextia Evidence.

Each row retains the native publication and exact native `state`/`reason`.
Operational focus outcomes `ok`, `partial` and `empty` carry the native status and
reason explicitly. `not_on_card` means no stored publication link on this capture;
it does not establish article absence. Query failures, unqueried sources,
annotation results and incomplete coverage remain separate original observations.
Missing captured identity prerequisites refuse interpretation explicitly.

The finite live cohort caches original publication explanations once per exact
request. Human and compact agent access share that detached result. Typed
projection, equality, membership, paging and ordering select original members;
they do not rewrite scientific outcomes, source scope or original alternatives.
Years retain native text values and missing scalars retain null.
