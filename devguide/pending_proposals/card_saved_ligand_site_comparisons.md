# Original saved ligand-site comparisons

The existing `CardView.save`, `AccessSelection.save` and `read_exploration`
signatures now support original ligand-site comparisons. Retained comparisons
require explicit `sabueso.card_exploration@3` and `site_exploration_binding@1`;
literal @1 and structural/coverage @2 retain their exact adapter declarations,
original readers and genuine producer capsules. No Card schema or packet mapping
changes. Saved @3 can mix the independently verified old and new adapters.

## Original interpretation and complete support

Frozen private predicates preserve `annotated_site_overlap@3`, canonical admission
`ligand_site_coordinate_scope@1`, source support, explanations and
`ligand_site_context_links@1`. They consume sealed captures through inert
`_HistoricalInput`; they never construct a Card, execute a current scientific
reader, acquire sources or register new credit. Expected records verify retained
output; they are not returned as new scientific knowledge. Generated producer
labels are distinguished from unchanged original source version fields.

Every retained value, raw/native result, unknown/partial admission, source input,
original list ordinal, structure-local context and quantity remains accessible.
Equal component labels do not identify physical occurrences or assemblies.
Source/request absence stays distinct from an unasked or failed source.

The original human table, selected/detail/question views, source explanations,
related destinations and return trail are retained. Machine lookup admits only
an exact saved projection/filter/membership/order request under the frozen
`ligand_site_access_selection@1` policy. A partial request is not an unrestricted
human topic; an unvisited question remains `not_saved`. `start_new()` explicitly
requests a fresh interpretation over the same captures.

Read-time validation checks the semantic result manifest, frozen scientific
decisions, source closure, typed cells, scope, schema, display selection and
actual originating item for explanation/related operations. Whole-file hashes
alone do not establish consistency, authenticity or source truth. Unknown
versions and coherently rebound scientific/selection/navigation changes fail.
Existing quantity codecs/field-unit bindings and source seals are unchanged.

## Finite preparation and accurate destination rules

The prototype exposed an implicit support-tree expansion: two saves of one
synthetic comparison grew from 55 to 873 views (about 1.36 to 10.47 MB), despite
no new exploration. The shared writer now records which views it generated for
automatic support and does not treat them as new explicit exploration on the
next save. Internal comparison-construction views are not user history. Explicit
subsequent exploration still enters the trail. Repeat-save guards cover literal,
structural and comparison routes; genuine older bundles remain readable.

Missing supplied comparison destinations previously displayed the generic
navigation adapter, despite having a comparison-specific link rule. Destinations
now retain the actual originating link rule, with a reproducing regression.
Comparison-related reading also returns to its original selected comparison view,
matching live navigation; generic item-detail behavior remains original for older
routes. Supplied-molecule transitions and foreign-focal rebinding are checked.

These changes bound automatic preparation; they do not promise optimal storage
or whole-workflow budgets. Source-rich real journeys and human acceptance still
need the broader workload review. Queries keep the previously qualified compact
source-membership delivery and atomic deferral.
