source, normalization, genomic correspondence, scientific default, Card schema
or SourceAssertion change. The [completion inventory](card_exploration_completion_0150.md)

## Shared occurrence and source scope

`explorer.view("sequence_changes")` and `answer("sequence_changes")` show one
retained Card annotation occurrence from `features_positional.natural_variant`
or `features_positional.mutagenesis`. The explicit
`sequence_change_projection@1` / `sequence_change_exploration@1` projection uses
the captured fields. Equal list occurrences keep different typed locations and
item references. Several native source statements can support one equal normalized
Card value; all stay visible. The projection never invents unique native-feature
correspondence, a source statement, an entity merge or an Evidence record.

Rows expose the original residue, the complete alternative list, the stated
missing/deletion flag, description, feature ID, sequence reference, endpoints and
their original modifiers. Alternatives remain one statement. `missing=True`,
`missing=False` and an unstated flag remain distinct. Unrecognized values retain
their full original input and an explicit cell state. A stated protein position
never joins a population genomic variant by equal residue number or a shared label.

`annotation_item_support@1` retains exact subject/field/value support and contextual
or missing references. Source conflicts and alternatives remain in coverage and
mandatory interpretation. The existing `annotation_structure_request@1` prepares
only its supported original structural question, including native molecule,
modifiers and sequence revision checks. Browsing or selecting this topic executes
no structural/tissue reader, alignment, acquisition or reference reconstruction.
An unplaced or unsupported annotation remains inspectable.

`access.select("sequence_changes")`. Both support typed equality, exact membership,
projection and scalar ordering under `sequence_change_selection@1`; the alternative
list supports exact list equality and is not sortable. Filtering keeps the full
underlying result and permits opening an original hidden item.

`open()` follows the original captured annotation, `explain()` opens every matching
source record, and `related()` reuses the existing original source/input links and
explicit structural question. Back routes retain the initiating selection and
question. Compact frames retain scope, original axis, substitution meaning, source
membership and mapping-lineage limits together. Response-budget deferral removes
values and their mandatory interpretation atomically while preserving typed states
and inspectable locations.

mutagenesis annotation. Positive mutagenesis, duplicate, missing/false/deletion,
