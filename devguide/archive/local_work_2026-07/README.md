### Reuse tests and data with the right scope

The archived tests are valuable acceptance scenarios, especially exclusive intake
modes, digest mismatch, missing-versus-zero tracks, exact sequence candidates,
partial per-structure failures, preservation of distinct measurements, predicted
versus experimental context and separate intervention modalities. Rewrite relevant
scenarios against current APIs; do not restore obsolete imports or fabricate
source fidelity from their synthetic payloads.

The HK2 snapshot stores mapped `field`/`value` records with source identifiers and
historical retrieval declarations, not native response envelopes. None of its
21,555 top-level evidence records has a `raw`, `payload`, `record` or `response`
field. Its 17,423 AlphaMissense records explain most of the stored observation
count and make it useful for card-size/report benchmarks. Nested values sometimes
retain source-shaped fragments; these do not reconstruct original download bytes,
pagination, hashes or execution receipts. The 188 ligand references are candidate
identifiers for explicit current resolution, not admissible current cards.

Do not restore the old EvidenceStore/schema, generic reference-card identity,
monolithic acquisition/orchestrator or duplicate current source clients. Shared
HTTP already supplies retries, archive identity and paced concurrent requests.
Current governance also supersedes old direct GO, SCOPe/TED, GTEx-expression and
M-CSA decisions where their old behavior conflicts with maintained policy.
