## Observation and boundary

An explicitly synthetic foreign subject in the public P52270/1SUX fixture was
admitted as focal by Card/Deck inventories, explanation, human/agent exploration
and the regional question. Exact original-parent navigation already refused it.
The structures packet projection delegates to those same native views and also
requires a versioned correction. No source query or private pilot is involved.

`experimental_structure_admission@1` scopes retained declarations. The canonical
Card id must explicitly name a UniProt protein. The retained `identifiers.uniprot`
must agree with that id and have no recorded identity conflict (including a
conflict recorded at the value path). A missing/null identifier or unavailable
identity-conflict context remains unplaced, consistently with original-parent
navigation. No identifier is inferred from relationships or source labels.
The relationship's subject must equal that exact declared subject. Missing,
foreign, opaque or conflicting anchors remain **unplaced**, with distinct reasons.
An alias is not resolved by name, similarity, equal coordinates or a shared PDB.

This is a referential admission basis, `exact_declared_card_subject`, with scope
`relationship_declaration_only`. It is not source authentication, entity merging,
sequence validation, reconstruction of an original mapping or coordinate
admission. Original source assertions are retained as support, including explicit
missing-record states; this rule never manufactures support. In particular,
admitting a declaration does not certify that a retained residue range belongs
to the current sequence. Coordinate questions must retain their own original
identity/reference/numbering prerequisites. Their broader review still belongs
to the structural integration route in #148.
The concrete [experimental coordinate follow-up](experimental_structure_coordinates.md)
is tracked in #170: a selected sequence contradicting original support can still
receive a positive regional projection. Declaration admission does not fix it.

## Current contracts

- `Card.structures()` returns admitted `items`, separate fragment `excluded`,
  named `admission_rule`, and `unplaced` original declarations.
- `structure_inventory@2` applies the same admission before qualifiers are
  interpreted. Malformed qualifiers on an unplaced declaration never become a
  focal state. State/coverage thresholds and explicit substitution maps retain
  their existing meanings. Every admitted row carries its admission basis;
  unplaced declarations are indexed by Card id, without entering groups or
  shared substitutions. Duplicate Card ids are refused before grouping because
  that output cannot address separate snapshots under one id.
- Unplaced records retain the exact relationship, reason, decision and typed
  original relationship/source locations. Available source records are linked,
  missing ones remain explicit. A missing Card pin does not acquire an invented
  address. Nothing is removed from the Card.
- `structure_inventory_explanation@3` keeps exact relationship selection and
  explicit reference-only ambiguity. An exact unplaced selection exposes its
  original relationship-level support, with no focal item/group. A foreign
  declaration sharing an admitted or excluded fragment's PDB label cannot change
  that sibling's membership.
- Human and compact agent access share one captured native inventory. Mandatory
  machine interpretation carries admission. Every compact page names whole-capture
  inventory/unplaced/fragment counts and a bounded `exclusions_location`, independently
  of display filters; human `exclusions` retains unplaced
  reasons and original explanations. The preview identifies unplaced declarations
  explicitly. An inapplicable or identity-less request has `not_evaluated` admission,
  null counts/rule/inspection link and its original prerequisite reason; unknown
  scope is not zero declarations. Live and inert saved pages share that behavior.
  Regional requests inherit the same admitted set, while sequence
  coverage remains distinct from observed-coordinate completeness.
- `packet_aspects@13` uses the corrected direct and comparative inventories and
  names the admission rule. Full/index terms include the original declarations,
  unplaced support and exact retained identifier dependency. Mapping @12 and
  earlier reconstruction use original declaration semantics; no historical
  packet payload or pin is rewritten.

Counts enumerate retained declarations, not independent experiments, PDB entries
or Evidence. An empty admitted set with unplaced declarations is not a finding
that the protein has no structure.
