# Quaternary interpretation admission

The #148 continuation audited the native computed readers before exposing their
findings through human/agent exploration. At `b4f7d31`, three synthetic cases
reproduced defects: a foreign-subject interface classified as focal homomeric;
missing coverage treated as a non-fragment complex; and an uncertain family
endpoint compared as an exact position. Tests also reproduce declaration loss
under a dictionary keyed by PDB label and selection of only the first homomeric
interface. The original quaternary source pane does not execute those classifiers.

## Corrected current interpretation

Development defaults use `interface_partner_class@2`,
`interface_site_agreement@3`, `quaternary_declaration_scope@1`,
`interface_coordinate_scope@1` and `oligomer_explanation@3`.

The canonical reference reuses the existing source-bound admission under
`ligand_site_coordinate_scope@1`, preserving its actual rule identity and inputs.
Reference identity/sequence/length, source assertions, modifiers, range bounds,
numbering and stated revisions remain explicit. Missing revisions do not establish
agreement. Original PDBe-KB interface endpoints are checked against the retained
mapped coordinates; alternate unsupported native shapes stay uncomputed.
The original native partner accession and observed PDB references must also bind
the retained declaration for classification. Contradictory partner labels cannot
become a homomeric or heteromeric finding while coordinate admission refuses them.

Every original structure declaration remains separate by relationship ID, even
with the same PDB reference. Foreign subjects remain visible as exclusions or
uncomputed interface context. Source records must bind the actual focal declaration;
RCSB structure-subject assertions additionally require their explicit original
structure/focal-protein tuple and matching record identity. This is a source-record
binding, never an entity merge. Unrecorded per-qualifier mapping lineage is not
reconstructed or claimed as direct source verification.

Missing, invalid or conflicting coverage cannot establish a non-fragment complex.
A qualified chimera declaration does not depend on a disputed coverage value;
its original alternatives still remain visible. Conflicting declaration bases
for one PDB reference are not resolved by list order. Distinct structure witnesses
and unknown contexts remain explicit.

Every focal self-interface/family-annotation pair has its own original relationship
ID and field ordinal, including repeated annotation descriptions/positions. A
qualified positive does not erase other uncomputed pairs. Empty computed residue
sets differ from `None`; no geometry, assembly projection, affinity or biological
relevance is inferred. Explanations retain actual paired inputs, original source
records, field alternatives and source/request/quality context. Readers do not
acquire data, create assertions, import conclusions or credit new source execution.
