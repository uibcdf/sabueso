## New retained-input interpretation

`experimental_structure_coordinates@1` requires exact original canonical support
from `ligand_site_coordinate_scope@1`, explicit declaration admission, original
RCSB source/object binding and retained polymer input. It validates focal source
alignment bounds and bijection, entity sequences, distinct chain identities,
complete instance scope and feature positions before projecting. The projected
ranges, coverage, entity/chimera membership, mutations/differences, observations,
author numbering and secondary assignments must match the retained qualifiers.
Conflicting positional qualifiers cannot authorize a comparison. An incomplete
chain invalidates that declaration conservatively; separate supported declarations
remain comparable. There is no new alignment, geometry or SourceAssertion.

The source envelope names the PDB entry, while its asserted relationship names the
protein and PDB. Both scopes are checked. `structure_reference_revision` remains
`not_stated`: an explicit accession axis is not an assertion that independent
source/reference revisions are equal. This named read-time interpretation does
not reconstruct an unrecorded original mapper invocation. Historical inputs
without raw polymer inputs stay uncomputed; do not backfill them from current
selected fields or normalized qualifiers.

`structure_state@3`, inventory `@3` and explanation `@4` carry this policy.
Unsupported positional fields are omitted only from private computation inputs;
original Card/source bytes stay untouched. Regional completeness and sequence
comparisons are `None`, not zero or a negative finding. The retained valid coverage
fraction/class remains the recorded normalized ratio used for fragment filtering; it
does not establish completeness against a changed current reference. No state
or shared-substitution group includes a declaration without admitted coordinates.
Every uncomputed row has its exact relationship id and reason.
Native Card/Deck region requests also refuse positions outside the admitted
original reference rather than calling them missing residues. The separate
[#171 allocation issue](https://github.com/uibcdf/sabueso/issues/171) covers range
expansion that precedes this semantic check; this correction does not claim to
bound that earlier normalization.
Explicit residue maps require a named original source-backed target Card, known
source Cards, valid source/target bounds and one-to-one position correspondence.
Nonidentity self-maps are refused. Inventory @3 retains the exact supplied maps
and target pin as derivation parameters; those caller inputs never assert identity
or an independently established alignment. Unsupported mapped substitutions remain
`None`, not an empty list. Regions naming absent Cards are refused rather than
silently ignored.

Human and agent results share the computation and mandatory per-row
`coordinate_scope`. A regional result with retained rows but no comparable row
reports `not_computed`. Request-level missing prerequisites still report
`missing_prerequisite`. Original declaration/source navigation remains available.
`packet_aspects@14` adds the canonical field support and names the new rules in
full/index outputs; historical @13 and earlier projections remain unchanged.

New saved readers use the separate frozen history @4 predicate family, original
retained inputs and no current mapper/scientific engine. Previous history @1/@2/@3
and their genuine producer artifacts keep their original meanings. The unchanged
synthetic explanation @3 fixture comes from fully qualified `5b9de48`; it is not a
new writer pretending to produce an old result. This view-only change modifies no
stored Card schema or migration.
