## Frozen receiving predicate

`sabueso/core/_source_active_site_selection_v1.py` freezes the existing
`source_active_site_selection@1` filter over `residue_annotations@1` inputs.
It reads only original fields and SourceAssertions: exact item/field/subject
support, normalized-value precedence, declared canonical axis/indexing, exact
in-bounds intervals and the original stated-revision mismatch policy. Missing
revisions are not inferred equal. Compound/fuzzy/foreign/unsupported annotations
retain their original nonmapped meaning. Literal dotted field traversal is
preserved. Sorted interval union produces the original ascending unique positions
without constructing every residue row or duplicating annotations per position.

Both receivers require that exact typed selection before trusting reported
regional outputs or composition inputs. Both examples also bind their original
axis/indexing and no-cross-protein-correspondence declarations. The new predicate is independent of current
residue, support and value helpers; its expected positions are verifier inputs,
never replacement output or new SourceAssertions. Reading adds no source credit.

## Compatibility and limits

This enforces a condition already declared by dossier criteria @1/@2 and
comparison selection @1. No formats, science rules, stored Card schema,
coordinates, values, source pins or original producer labels change. Legitimate
dossier @1/@2/@3 reports retain their own structural/assay meanings; comparison
@1 has no residue context and stays on that branch. Explicit legacy production
tests are current-producer policy replays, not purported earlier artifacts.
A genuine pre-fix @3 ordinary artifact from `fac09b0` still reads; its coherent
modified copy now fails at the selection guard without altering either source
database or runtime record.

An empty selection means no positions qualified under that original predicate.
It does not establish absent active sites or complete coordinate coverage; the
original dossier criterion remains unknown for an empty selection. This does not
upgrade unsupported historical annotations or claim full scientific validation of
every other comparison output. A future changed selector needs a new named
policy; it must not reinterpret this original selection silently.
