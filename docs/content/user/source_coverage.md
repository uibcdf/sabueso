# Source coverage

Development FDA OOPD now reads original public detailed-page artifacts on
requested page subjects. Designation and approval tables, procedural dates, blank
exclusivity fields, N/A and sponsor qualifiers stay independent. The HTML does not
echo cfgridkey; the requested URL or supplied declaration binds a page, not a stable
designation, product or protein ID. No target/modality defaults, clinical conclusion,
search completeness or automatic card enrichment. The later shared-transport live
check receives HTTP 404; native supplied-artifact reading is qualified separately
from restored automated access.

Development TTD retains original target listing declarations on exact source IDs;
entry names/placeholders and type labels do not infer protein identity or a clinical
finding. Data terms remain NOT-STATED with unknown sharing. Development iPTMnet
reads all original HTML substrate-group rows and hidden references under database
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, without canonical residue projection or confidence recalculation.

Development BRENDA reads source EC-class names and descriptions, preserving
native RDF terms and every received solution on an independent EC subject.
This does not establish an individual protein's catalytic activity or kinetics.
ASD remains evaluating. TTD now has a scoped native listing reader; its unknown
use/sharing conditions are recorded in the source catalog.

Development Pharos/TCRD reads exact native target identity and five selected fields,
including the provider TDL literal, without calculated ranking or aggregate

from full 24Q4-v1 Model.csv, not gene dependency or essentiality matrices. Both
preserve original source support and independent revision/terms scope; the selected
DepMap article states CC BY 4.0, while a separate Pharos output grant is unstated.

`Card.knowledge_state()` describes scientific knowledge/coverage on a protein
card. Acquisition traces describe observed requests and their outcomes. Inspect
both, together with enrichment reports and deck membership/exclusions. `not_stated`,
`not_queried`, `unavailable`, `partial` and conflicting knowledge need different
interpretations; none automatically establishes a biological negative.

## Reuse and citations

Use {doc}`terms` to inspect allowed use and obligations. Original literature
citations and resource-description citations remain distinct; bibliography does
not license downloaded content. Unknown versions or incomplete citations should
remain explicit. Exact stored references identify what you used, and original
runtime records identify the observed execution; preserve both for another reader.
